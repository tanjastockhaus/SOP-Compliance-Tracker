SOP Compliance

Welcome to the Wep-Application SOP Compliance. This tool is designed to improve data acquiring in an out of hospital setting. Paramedics or EMTs don't have much time between missions to fill out complicated forms. So this tool was developed to allow a fast and simple data acquisition. The data used are time-stamps medical personal can look up in their documentation. Various standards or spezific parts of guidelines are examined for compliance.

STEMI

For example, the study investigates the time interval between initial patient contact and a 12-lead ECG in patients with chest pain. The ACS guideline specifies a maximum of 10 minutes.

STROKE

Also under investigation is the time interval between initial patient contact and a CT in patients with suspected stroke. The Stroke guideline specifies a maximum of 25 minutes between Door(Hospital)-to-CT. The recommendations for on scene time are about 15 minutes as well as a transport duration up to 30 min. A mission is flagged as compliant if the duration between the on_scene time and the ct are less than or equal to 70 minutes.

TRAUMA

The third research objective is the care of trauma patients. The study investigates the time interval between initial patient contact and the arrival in a trauma bay. The Trauma guideline specifies a maximum of 60 minutes.
